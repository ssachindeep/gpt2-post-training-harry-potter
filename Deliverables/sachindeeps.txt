⏺ On warmup steps: with a cosine LR scheduler, the learning rate starts at its
  peak value (5e-5) on step 0 and decays from there. Warmup adds a short linear
  ramp-up phase (e.g. first 100 steps go from 0 → 5e-5), which stabilizes early
  training because the model weights are randomly initialized relative to the
  new task and large gradients in step 1 can cause instability. For fine-tuning
  a pretrained model like GPT-2 (weights are already good), the risk is lower,
  so skipping warmup is a reasonable call. Just flagging it as a known tradeoff
  — not a blocker.
