Starting from raw inputs, we feed the data to byte pair encoding. Through byte pair encoding, from the GPT2 vocabulary we map the token to their respective numeric token id. The numeric token id are mapped using embedding matrix, to embedding vector. Next we add on positional embedding vector to it. Together they are fed to the transformer block. Then these vectors are fed to the learned parameters of W_Q, W_K, W_V (which are updated in our case during post training with Harry Potter corpus) at the same time. After the given attention block, embedding vectors are passed through layer normalization. And after passing layer normalization is passed through the other 11 MMHA blocks. After passing through the 12th block's layer normalization, the logits produced is softmaxed. The softmax is done for the full vocabulary logits. Based on temperature, we adjust the probability distribution. Further in order to select the top-k or top-p token, we may or may not select the token with highest probability.  Further this token is appended to the input sequence and passed through the model again and loops until end of limit is reached.

Before fine-tuning, the cross-entropy loss for dry-run was around 9.3883. During the first epoch, after about 1000 steps, it reduced substantially to around 4.1998, showing that the model was adapting to the Harry Potter corpus.
