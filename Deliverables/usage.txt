## 3. Local Inference Comparison

Command (base):
  python sample.py --prompt "Harry looked at" --max_new_tokens 50 --seed 42

Command (fine-tuned):
  python sample.py --prompt "Harry looked at" --max_new_tokens 50 --seed 42 \
    --checkpoint checkpoints/checkpoint-epoch1-step450

--- Base GPT-2 Output ---
Prompt   : Harry looked at
Generated: her and said "No I have nothing more to do than to take you to
           see the doctor and we'll get to it. I'll come back to tell you
           about the whole thing with you." She got her coat as she came in
           quickly and

--- Fine-tuned GPT-2 Output ---
Prompt   : Harry looked at
Generated: her and said, 'But I know Hagrid . . . Hagrid has been talking
           to my family. . . . I know what he is doing . . . . you see . .
           . Hagrid has told me, he is . .

--- Key Differences ---
1. Domain shift:
   - Base      : generic English narrative (doctor, coat, she came in)
   - Fine-tuned: Harry Potter characters (Hagrid, ellipsis style ". . .")

2. Step 0 top candidates:
   - Base      : ' the' (0.265), ' her' (0.229), ' him' (0.169)
   - Fine-tuned: ' Harry' (0.336), ' the' (0.135), ' Hermione' (0.087)
   → Fine-tuned model surfaces HP character names as top candidates

3. Token certainty — 'Hagrid' learned as a strong bigram:
   After token 'Hag', P('rid') = 1.000 in fine-tuned model
   (The model has fully memorised this character name token pair)

4. Dialogue style:
   - Base      : double-quote speech  ("No I have nothing...")
   - Fine-tuned: single-quote style   ('But I know Hagrid...')
   → Matches the Harry Potter book corpus dialogue convention
